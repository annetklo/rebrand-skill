02

Scope decision

The most important choice of the whole engagement: do we refine what exists, or start over? Every later design decision follows from this answer.

Chosen

Evolution

Refine the existing mark and wordmark. Same brand DNA, executed tighter and more consistently. Recognition stays fully intact; existing clients see a more mature version of the same brand.

Chosen

Revolution

A new mark and a new visual language. The brand DNA is translated afresh rather than polished. A bigger leap, a bigger risk, and the only route when the current identity can no longer carry the story.
