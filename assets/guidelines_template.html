04

Misuse

Eight things that are never allowed, regardless of context. Each example shows the chosen mark deliberately misapplied, rendered in the phase 4 stress test; as long as that render is missing, a neutral proxy shape stands in.

✕
Do not stretch or compress
✕
Do not pick your own colours: only the defined variants
✕
Do not add an outline
✕
No shadow, glow or filter
✕
Not on busy backgrounds without a calm backdrop
✕
Do not rotate or tilt
✕
Do not use semi-transparent as a watermark
✕
Never mix the old and new mark: the migration per carrier is atomic

Standards (WCAG 2.2): at least 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text (from 18.66px bold or 24px regular) and for UI components. A combination that only reaches 3:1 is therefore allowed for headings and buttons, not for body text.
